### Report

Wildfires in eastern Washington state forced the evacuation of 60,000 people in the Spokane area on Sunday while destroying at least 600 homes, businesses and other structures, authorities said. The fires in and around Spokane, the state’s second largest city, burned about 8.2 square miles, or roughly 21 square kilometers, over the weekend. They were among dozens of wildfires across the Western U.S. that stretched the ability of federal, state and local agencies to fight them, according to the Associated Press.

A total of about 390 square miles, equivalent to 1,000 square kilometers, has burned across Washington state. Some of the fires, including those in the Spokane area, have not been contained. Fire and law enforcement officials said there had been no reports of injuries or people unaccounted for, though they stressed it was early in the response effort. Maria Cantwell said during a news conference with federal, state and local officials in Spokane. “The next couple of days will be challenging.” Investigators had yet to determine how the blazes started.

Spokane was hardly the only place struggling to contain fires. Crews using bulldozers and helicopters continued for a 10th day to fight a fire that has burned nearly 525 square miles, about 1,360 square kilometers, of grassland in western Idaho and eastern Oregon. The area is home to cattle ranches, and authorities said the fire is threatening more than 600 homes and 800 other structures. The weather in the area was expected to remain sunny and dry well into the week, with temperatures rising to more than 90 degrees Fahrenheit, or 32 degrees Celsius, by Wednesday. Officials say that fire was caused by lightning.

In central Utah, a massive blaze more than doubled in size over the weekend, jumping from 16 square miles to about 56 square miles by Sunday morning. More than 100 cattle have been killed, according to local law enforcement. Officials called the fire “catastrophic” as heavy winds helped flames jump containment lines overnight. The fire is moving north toward Fillmore, a town of about 2,600 people and the seat of Millard County. Mandatory evacuations were ordered Sunday evening for the southern portion of Fillmore.

Washington Governor said he talked to federal officials about the fires.

### Report

The strike damaged 22 apartment buildings across four districts of the city, according to Fedorov’s assessment of the overnight onslaught (ap_india).

The weapons used are Soviet-era bombs retrofitted with guidance systems, capable of carrying up to 3,000 kilograms (6,600 pounds) of explosives. Russia has deployed these glide bombs to strike Ukrainian towns and cities since the full-scale invasion began more than four years ago, exploiting a critical gap in Ukraine’s defenses. Ukrainian officials acknowledge they have no effective countermeasures against the munitions, which allow Russian aircraft to release payloads from distances that keep them outside the range of Ukrainian air defense systems (ap_india).

He attributed the short supply to competing demands from the Iran war. “The world has Patriot missiles. What matters now is for our partners to make the political decision to provide the necessary packages,” Zelenskyy wrote on social media late Sunday. “Anti-ballistic missiles must protect people — not sit in stockpiles” (ap_india).

The request faces uncertainty in Washington. U.S. President Donald Trump said last Friday that a potential license for Ukraine to manufacture Patriots domestically remains under discussion and that he has not made a final decision, appearing to step back from an earlier commitment. Zelenskyy has floated an alternative proposal involving Elon Musk’s Starlink satellites to target ballistic missile launch sites inside Russia, though officials have not publicly indicated whether that option is under serious consideration (ap_india).

The Russian Defense Ministry claimed its forces struck four bulk carriers carrying cargo for the Ukrainian armed forces, three in the Black Sea and one in the port of Mykolaiv, without providing further details. Separately, the Russian-installed head of Crimea, Sergei Aksyonov, said a Ukrainian attack on the peninsula overnight killed three people and wounded two (ap_india).

The violence extended deeper into Russian territory. Ukrainian drones struck a major warehouse belonging to Wildberries, Russia’s largest online retailer, in the Vladimir region, causing a significant fire. The company said the facility caught fire after a drone attack, part of a campaign that has hit more than a dozen of its distribution hubs. Regional governors said Ukrainian drone strikes across Russia killed eight people in total (reuters_india).

In Moscow, Mayor Sergei Sobyanin on Sunday labeled a Saturday evening bombing at the upscale Balzi Rossi restaurant a “brutal terrorist act.” The blast killed three people, including the woman who carried the explosive device, and wounded at least 21. A security guard had reportedly refused the woman entry before the device detonated. Sobyanin said a probe was underway and vowed those responsible would be found, though authorities have not drawn connections to the war in Ukraine or previous attacks on Russian soil (ap_india).
